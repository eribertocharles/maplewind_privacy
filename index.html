Privacy Policy — Maplewind

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Maplewind is an on-device text-to-speech app. In short: Maplewind does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

What Maplewind does with your text

  • Your text, documents, and the audio made from them stay on your device. When you type text, import a document, or scan a page with the camera, that content is processed and stored locally on your iPhone. Speech is generated on-device; so are listening briefs.
  • Nothing you give Maplewind is ever uploaded. Maplewind does not send your text, imported documents, generated audio, briefs, highlights, or anything derived from them to us or to any third party. There is no server that receives your content.
  • You are in control. Recordings, documents, and highlights remain on your device until you delete them. Removing them in the app removes them from the device.

Camera

Maplewind uses the camera only when you choose to scan a page or a sign, so its text can be read aloud. Text recognition runs on your device; the image is not uploaded, and Maplewind does not retain the photograph after the text has been extracted. The camera is not accessed at any other time.

Documents you import

When you import a file, Maplewind copies it into its own private storage so it can be read and re-opened later. PDFs and EPUBs are kept in their original form so the app can show you the real page while it reads. These copies live only on your device and are deleted when you delete the document in the app. Maplewind reads the file's own modification date to keep its library tidy — no other file information is examined, and nothing outside the files you explicitly choose is accessed.

Network use

Maplewind works entirely on-device. The speech models it runs (Kokoro and its pronunciation model) and the listening-brief model ship inside the app itself, so a normal install and a normal day of use make no network requests at all.

The one exception: if a speech model is ever missing or damaged, Maplewind can re-download that model from its public hosting on Hugging Face. That is a one-time download of the model to your device. No text, documents, audio, briefs, or personal information is ever transmitted — data flows only to your device, never away from it.

Maplewind contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

No accounts, no tracking

  • No account or sign-in. Maplewind has no user accounts and asks for no personal information.
  • No tracking. Maplewind does not track you, does not use the Advertising Identifier, and shares no data with data brokers or advertising networks. It contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data storage and security

Your text, documents, generated audio, and highlights are stored in the app's private, sandboxed storage on your device, protected by iOS's standard app data protections. Because the data never leaves your device, its security is governed by your device's own protections (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, encryption at rest).

Children's privacy

Maplewind collects no personal information from anyone, including children — it does not knowingly collect data from children because it does not collect data at all. Maplewind is not directed to children.

Maplewind reads aloud whatever text you give it. What it says is determined entirely by the content you provide, and Maplewind does not review, filter, or moderate that content.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before the corresponding app version ships.
